🏆 Trophy Hunt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Trophy Hunt is a points game for viewers of the Gone To The Dogs YouTube channel. This policy explains what the leaderboard page does with your Google account information when you sign in.

What we access

When you tap Sign in, the page requests read-only access to your YouTube channel information (Google's youtube.readonly scope). At sign-in time the page reads:

  • Your YouTube channel ID
  • Your YouTube channel title

Nothing else. The page never reads your subscriptions, watch history, playlists, or any other Google account data, and it never posts, comments, or acts on YouTube as you.

Why we need it

Your channel ID is matched against the public game entries (your YouTube comments on contest videos) so the leaderboard can automatically show your points, streak, and per-video checkmarks after sign-in. Without it, the page cannot tell which player you are.

Where your data lives

  • The channel lookup happens in your own browser at sign-in time. The access token is revoked immediately after the one lookup.
  • Only your player handle is remembered, in your browser's own local storage, so the page recognizes you on your next visit. You can clear it any time with the sign-out chip.
  • There is no server: nothing is uploaded, stored, or processed anywhere else.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your Google account information to any third party, ever.

Children

Trophy Hunt is a general-audience game. If you are under 13, please have a parent or guardian sign in for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
