📜 HOW TO PLAY

🐶 Enter daily — Comment your dog's NAME on any Gone To The Dogs video. One scoring comment per video per day: 1 video = 10 pts · 2 videos = 25 pts · all 3 = 60 pts. Keep it clean — inappropriate names don't score.

💬 Say anything — Any comment on any Gone To The Dogs video → +1 pt, once a day. Get your name on the board just for showing up! Every day you comment grows your streak — 3 days in a row earns a bonus.

🔥 Streaks — Play consecutive days: 3 days +10 · 7 days +25 · 14 days +50 · 30 days +100. Miss a day and your streak resets.

📸 Photo bonus — Sign in, tap your name, and add a real photo of your dog → +25 pts every day.

🎬 Video bonus — send a real clip of your dog for the show → +50 pts every day. Real clips only, no stock or AI.

🧭 Explorer bonus — First scoring entry on any video → +10 pts, once per video. Check the Videos tab to see which videos still have your bonus waiting!

✅ Daily checklist — tap your name in the standings to see what's still up for grabs today. Clear the whole list in one day → +25 bonus.

👑 Top Dog — Most Season 1 points wins: a dedicated video about YOUR dog + your dog on our channel banner for the month!

📢 You must be subscribed to Gone To The Dogs to win.
